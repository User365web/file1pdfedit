Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-12

This Privacy Policy explains how File1pdfedit (“we”, “our”) handles information when you use https://www.file1pdfedit.theworkpc.com.

Your files

All PDF and image processing happens inside your web browser on your own device. Files you select are not uploaded to, stored on or viewed by our servers.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name or email address through the tools. If you contact us by email, we keep your message and address only to reply to you.

Like most websites, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may process technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We may use privacy-friendly analytics to count visits.

Cookies and advertising

We show ads provided by third-party advertising partners, which may include Google AdSense and Adsterra. These partners may use cookies, web beacons and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies at aboutads.info.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you may be shown a consent message so you can choose how your data is used for advertising.

Third-party libraries

The tools load open-source libraries (pdf-lib, PDF.js and JSZip) from the cdnjs content delivery network when you use a tool. Your browser contacts that network to download the code. Your documents are not sent to it.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we hold almost no personal data, there is usually little to delete, but you can contact us at any time.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email your-email@example.com or visit the contact page.
