About File1pdfedit

File1pdfedit is a collection of free online PDF tools that work entirely in your web browser.

Why we built it

Many PDF websites ask you to upload private documents to a server, create an account or pay to remove a watermark. We wanted a simple alternative: open the page, drop a file, get the result.

How it works

When you use a tool, your browser reads the file and does the work locally with open-source JavaScript libraries. Your document is never sent to our servers, which makes the tools fast and keeps sensitive files private.

What you can do

  • Compress PDF - Reduce PDF file size for easy sharing.
  • Image to PDF - Turn JPG, PNG and WebP images into a PDF.
  • Merge PDF - Combine multiple PDFs into one file.
  • Split PDF - Extract selected pages into a new PDF.
  • Rotate PDF - Fix pages that are sideways or upside down.
  • PDF Page Counter - Check how many pages your PDF contains.
  • PDF Info - View page count, size and document properties.
  • Delete PDF Pages - Remove unwanted pages from a PDF.
  • PDF to JPG - Convert PDF pages to JPG or PNG images.
  • Add Page Numbers - Number every page of your PDF.
  • Add Watermark - Stamp text such as DRAFT on every page.
  • Edit PDF Metadata - Change title, author, subject and keywords.

How the site is funded

File1pdfedit is free to use. We display advertising to cover the time it takes to build and maintain the tools. See our Privacy Policy for details.
