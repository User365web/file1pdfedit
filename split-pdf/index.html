Split PDF

Extract selected pages into a new PDF, or save every page as its own file.

✂️Drop a PDF here to split itChoose fileor drag and drop. PDF files only

    Private: your files are processed on your device and never uploaded.

    How to use Split PDF

    1. Add your PDFDrop a PDF onto the box.
    2. Choose pagesType ranges such as 1-3, 5, 8- or switch to one file per page.
    3. DownloadPress Split PDF and save the new file or ZIP.

    Split PDF guide and tips

    Type page numbers separated by commas. A dash means a range, so 1-3, 7 gives pages 1, 2, 3 and 7. An open range such as 8- means page 8 to the end.

    Pages are extracted in the order you type them, so 5, 1 puts page 5 before page 1 in the new file.

    Pick Save every page as its own PDF to get a ZIP file with one PDF per page.

    Split PDF FAQ

    Can I split a PDF into single pages?

    Yes. Choose the one-file-per-page option and you will get a ZIP file containing every page as a separate PDF.

    Is the original file changed?

    No. Your original file is never modified. You download a new file.

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. Everything happens inside your browser on your own device. Your PDFs and images are never sent to our servers, so your documents stay private.

    Is this tool really free?

    Yes. It is free to use with no sign-up, no watermark and no daily limit. The site is supported by advertising.
