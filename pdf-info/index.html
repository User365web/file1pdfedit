    1. Add your PDFDrop one or more PDF files onto the box.
    2. Read the detailsProperties appear instantly below the box.

    PDF Info guide and tips

    PDF Info shows the page count, file size, paper size and orientation, PDF version, whether the file is encrypted, and the stored title, author, subject, keywords, creator, producer, creation date and modification date.

    Use it to check which program created a file, to confirm a page is A4 or Letter, or to see what personal information a PDF stores before you share it.

    PDF Info FAQ

    What does the producer field mean?

    It names the software that produced the PDF file, for example a word processor or a scanner program.

    Why are some fields empty?

    Not every PDF stores every property. Many files have no title, author or keywords.

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. Everything happens inside your browser on your own device. Your PDFs and images are never sent to our servers, so your documents stay private.

    Is this tool really free?

    Yes. It is free to use with no sign-up, no watermark and no daily limit. The site is supported by advertising.
