Delete PDF Pages

Remove blank, duplicate or unwanted pages from a PDF.

🗑️Drop a PDF here to remove pagesChoose fileor drag and drop. PDF files only

    Private: your files are processed on your device and never uploaded.

    How to use Delete PDF Pages

    1. Add your PDFDrop the PDF onto the box.
    2. Type pages to deleteUse numbers and ranges such as 2, 4-6.
    3. DownloadPress Delete pages and save the shorter PDF.

    Delete PDF Pages guide and tips

    Check the page numbers in your PDF viewer first, then type them in the box. Ranges and single pages can be mixed, for example 1, 3-4, 9-.

    At least one page must remain. Your original file is never changed.

    Delete PDF Pages FAQ

    Can I undo a deletion?

    Your original file is untouched, so just start again with it. The new file has the pages removed permanently.

    Can I delete the last page only?

    Yes. Type the last page number, or type a range such as 10- to remove everything from page 10 onward.

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. Everything happens inside your browser on your own device. Your PDFs and images are never sent to our servers, so your documents stay private.
