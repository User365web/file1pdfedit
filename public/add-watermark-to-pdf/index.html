    How to use Add Watermark

    1. Add your PDFDrop the PDF onto the box.
    2. Set the textType the watermark and choose layout, size and opacity.
    3. DownloadPress Add watermark and save the file.

    Add Watermark guide and tips

    A diagonal watermark is hard to ignore and works well for DRAFT or SAMPLE. A horizontal watermark looks calmer and suits company names.

    Light opacity keeps the page readable. Increase it if the watermark is hard to see on busy pages.

    Watermark text supports Latin letters, numbers and common symbols.

    Add Watermark FAQ

    Can the watermark be removed later?

    The watermark is drawn onto the page, so it is not meant to be switched off. Keep your original file if you need a clean copy.

    Can I use my own logo?

    This tool adds text watermarks only. Image watermarks are not available yet.

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. Everything happens inside your browser on your own device. Your PDFs and images are never sent to our servers, so your documents stay private.
