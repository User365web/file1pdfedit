    How to use Edit PDF Metadata

    1. Add your PDFDrop the PDF onto the box. Current values load automatically.
    2. Edit the fieldsChange the title, author, subject or keywords.
    3. DownloadPress Save properties and save the updated file.

    Edit PDF Metadata guide and tips

    The title is what many PDF viewers and browsers show in the tab or window bar, so a clear title makes your file look professional and easier to find.

    Clear the Author field before sharing a document if you do not want your name stored inside the file.

    Keywords are separated with commas.

    Edit PDF Metadata FAQ

    Does this change the pages?

    No. Only the document properties are changed. Page content stays exactly the same.

    Can I remove all metadata?

    Empty the four fields and save. The producer and creator fields written by other software are not shown here, so check them with PDF Info.

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. Everything happens inside your browser on your own device. Your PDFs and images are never sent to our servers, so your documents stay private.

    Yes. It is free to use with no sign-up, no watermark and no daily limit. The site is supported by advertising.
